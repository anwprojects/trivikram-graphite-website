MATERIALS

The right material
for the right
application.

Material selection decides how a graphite component behaves in service. We help you choose a material that suits the job.

01 / MATERIAL SELECTION

What we consider.

These are the questions we work through with you before recommending a material.

01

Graphite grade

Different grades behave differently in service. The grade should follow from the metal, the temperature and the equipment.

02

Density

Density influences how a component wears and how it handles molten metal over repeated use.

03

Purity

Where the melt must stay clean, as with precious metals, the purity of the graphite matters.

04

Grain structure

Finer, more uniform graphite can be machined to finer detail and a smoother surface.

05

Application requirements

The machine, the cycle and the expected service life decide which trade-offs make sense.

02 / MATERIALS WE WORK WITH

Graphite in
our products.

Isostatic HD Graphite

Isostatically pressed graphite is formed under equal pressure from all directions, which gives a fine and uniform structure. We use it where detail and consistency matter most.

HD Graphite

High-density graphite used for consumables that work in direct contact with molten metal.

USED IN

Extruded Graphite

03 / SOURCING

We source the
material. We make
the part.

Trivikram Graphite does not manufacture base graphite. We source specialised graphite materials and machine them into finished components in our own unit.

If your application calls for a specific grade, or you are unsure which material is appropriate, tell us about the metal, the equipment and the operating conditions. We will suggest a suitable material and confirm its details with your enquiry.

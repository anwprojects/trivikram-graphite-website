About us

Building on graphite,expanding horizons.

Trivikram Graphite Private Limited. Formerly Perfect Graphite & Electricals.

01 / Our journey

A family business, a new chapter.

Perfect Graphite & Electricals was founded by Mr. Ganapathy Srinivasan in 2005 and has been a trusted name in the industry for two decades. Trivikram Graphite Pvt. Ltd. is the new chapter in our family’s dedication to providing high-quality graphite products.

Now joined by his son, Mr. Rishikesh Ganapathy, the directors bring together deep industry experience, a fresh perspective and a focus on innovation.

We consistently deliver graphite consumables including graphite crucibles, stopper rods, continuous casting dies and mandrels. We have also supplied specialised graphite products for aerospace applications.

02 / What we do

Manufacturerand supplier.

We are clear about what we make ourselves and what we source.

What we manufacture

Graphite consumables and components, machined in our own unit.

Crucibles, stopper rods, continuous casting dies, mandrels and ingot moulds, made to suit the machine and the application.

What we source

The graphite material our components are made from.

We do not produce the base graphite. We source isostatic HD and extruded graphite and select a suitable material for each requirement.

What remains unchanged

Our dedication to the same quality of graphite products our customers have relied on.

What is new

  • Improved efficiencyStreamlined processes for faster delivery and better order fulfilment.
  • Enhanced communicationImproved responsiveness and personalised customer support.
  • Innovative solutionsExploring new product lines and technologies to meet evolving needs.

03 / Company and leadership

Two decades ingraphite. A newchapter.

Perfect Graphite & Electricals was founded by Mr. Ganapathy Srinivasan in 2005. Trivikram Graphite Private Limited carries that work forward, now joined by his son, Mr. Rishikesh Ganapathy.

Director

Ganapathy Srinivasan

Mr. Ganapathy brings over two decades of experience in procurement and more than two and a half decades of entrepreneurial leadership. He served as a Purchase Manager at Sylvex Cables for 12 years, followed by a 14-year tenure in the same role at Uni Deritend Ltd, manufacturers of investment castings. In the early 2000s he moved into entrepreneurship, first trading and later establishing his own manufacturing unit for graphite consumables. He has supported the jewellery industry in import substitution by locally manufacturing OEM products.

Managing Director

Rishikesh Ganapathy

Mr. Rishikesh is a qualified Chartered Accountant, Cost Accountant and Company Secretary, with over 4 years of consulting experience in the manufacturing industry and more than 10 years in audit and risk roles within leading insurance companies. In his previous roles he led automation initiatives, helping organisations move to digital audits and manage risk on a near real time basis. He is passionate about driving innovation and contributing to the growth of Trivikram Graphite.
